Module 5 · Answerability

Positive class: answerable from the official corpus. A substantive answer or source excerpt counts as an attempt regardless of correctness. An explicit refusal counts as negative. For the binary Qwen decision, an instruction echo with no substantive answer counts as negative; it is still a format error in QA grading. Seven emergency/directory routing checks are outside the live-app binary task.

Module 6 · Fifteen difficult cases

1. Live app · Centre mein kam se kam kitne beds hone chahiye?

Expected evidence: OSC p20 · minimum five beds

SOP ke mutabik har OSC mein temporary shelter ke liye kam se kam 5 beds hone chahiye.
One_Stop_Centre_SOP_English.pdf · PDF p. 20

The numerical question asks for a capacity standard. The reply gives the lower bound.

2. Live app · Aaj raat nearest OSC mein bed khaali hai?

Expected evidence: Static documents lack current occupancy

Live availability ya personal case status in documents mein nahi hai. Service se directly contact karein.
Mission Shakti

The same bed vocabulary appears as in the capacity question, but aaj raat changes the information needed.

3. Live app · Meri complaint aaj close ho gayi kya?

Expected evidence: Individual complaint status unavailable

Live availability ya personal case status in documents mein nahi hai. Service se directly contact karein.
Mission Shakti

The date word aaj and the personal complaint require an individual case record.

4. Live app · How long do they normally allow someone to sleep at a Sakhi centre?

Expected evidence: OSC p19 · usual five days and conditional extensions

Usually five days. If relocation to Shakti Sadan is infeasible, the administrator can extend the stay to ten days case by case. The DNO/DPO can authorize up to ten further days.
One_Stop_Centre_SOP_English.pdf · PDF p. 19

Sleep at a Sakhi centre expresses shelter duration indirectly. The response preserves the conditions on extensions.
